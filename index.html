About MANMARZ Group

One group. Six industries. A single standard of quality.

Built from the ground up in Wa, with an office in Accra, working across Ghana.

MANMARZ Group Limited began as a construction and general merchant company and has grown into a diversified group capable of taking on projects across construction, mining, oil & gas, and agriculture. That breadth means we can move materials, machinery, and expertise between divisions to keep every project on schedule and on budget.

Under the leadership of our General Director, Marzuk Yahaya, we've built a team of experienced professionals who understand both the technical demands of heavy industry and the realities of doing business from the Upper West Region to Accra and beyond.

Whatever the sector, our approach stays the same: understand the client's goals, plan carefully, and execute with integrity, safety, and quality at every stage.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does MANMARZ Group Limited offer?
We offer building construction, road construction, civil and earthworks, mining and quarry operations, oil and gas support services, agriculture and agribusiness, project management, and general supplies and general merchant trading.
Where is MANMARZ Group Limited based?
We're headquartered at Wa Airport in the Upper West Region of Ghana, with a second office in Accra, and we serve clients across the country.
Does MANMARZ Group operate in mining and quarrying?
Yes. Our mining division covers site assessment, extraction support, and aggregate supply, working alongside our construction and civil works teams.
Does MANMARZ Group provide oil and gas services?
Yes. We support oil and gas sector clients with logistics, site infrastructure, civil works, and general supply services.
Is MANMARZ Group involved in agriculture?
Yes. Our agribusiness division supports farm infrastructure, land development, and agricultural supply chains in the Upper West Region and beyond.
